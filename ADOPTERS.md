# Adopters

Use this page to share how you use Kanivet. Maintainers include adopter entries with the named party's permission.

To share your use, open a PR adding a short entry with:

- the individual, team, or organization name you have permission to publish;
- the workflow Kanivet supports and whether use is evaluation, development, or production;
- the Kanivet version and relevant environment, without sensitive infrastructure details;
- an optional public story or contact link, with permission.

You do not need to publish cluster names, credentials, usage volumes, or a contact email. Maintainers may ask for confirmation before merging an entry. Request updates or removal through a PR or issue. Listing an adopter does not imply endorsement or paid support.
