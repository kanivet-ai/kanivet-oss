# Code of conduct

Kanivet adopts the standards and scope of the [CNCF Community Code of Conduct v1.3](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md). Everyone participating in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and project events is expected to follow it. This adoption does not imply CNCF membership.

Be respectful, welcoming, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of private information without permission, and retaliation against reporters are unacceptable.

## Project reporting and enforcement

Email [Nuno Morais](mailto:nuno.mcvmorais@gmail.com) at **nuno.mcvmorais@gmail.com** with the subject `Kanivet conduct report`. Include the relevant context and links, and explain any concerns about sharing information with other maintainers. Do not post a confidential conduct report in a public issue or security advisory.

For an alternate contact, including reports concerning Nuno, email [Miguel Guerreiro](mailto:oliveira.guerreiro@tecnico.ulisboa.pt) at **oliveira.guerreiro@tecnico.ulisboa.pt** with the subject `Kanivet conduct report`. Choose a contact who is not involved in the incident. You may also use GitHub's platform reporting tools for abusive content.

Responders aim to acknowledge reports within five business days, handle information only with people needed to resolve the incident, and explain the next steps. A responder implicated in a report must recuse themselves. Responses should be proportionate and may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or removal from project participation. The affected person may request review by an uninvolved responder. Protect reporters from retaliation and do not promise absolute confidentiality where safety or legal obligations require otherwise.

The upstream CNCF code credits the [Contributor Covenant 2.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/). This project-specific reporting process applies while Kanivet is independent; any future CNCF onboarding must reconcile reporting and jurisdiction with CNCF.
